# Nasdaq Nordic screener and instrument captures

- Captured on 2026-09-16 with plain GETs (user agent `Equibles/1.0`, `Accept: application/json`, no cookies) from `https://api.nasdaq.com`.
- `screener-shares.STO.MAIN_MARKET.sample.json` is the Stockholm main-market reply of `/api/nordic/screener/shares?category=MAIN_MARKET&market=STO&tableonly=false` (411 rows that day, one quoted in EUR) trimmed to 10 verbatim rows: AAK, ASSA B, ATCO A, ATCO B, ALIV SDB, BESQAB PREF B, ERIC B, INVE B, VOLV B and the EUR-quoted VSURE. `pagination.total` is rewritten to the kept count; every other value is unchanged. Share classes and depositary lines carry a space in `symbol`; `micCode` is empty on every row.
- `screener-shares.STO.FIRST_NORTH.sample.json` is the First North reply (`category=FIRST_NORTH`, 330 rows that day) trimmed to its first 4 rows the same way.
- `instrument-info.VOLV-B.json`, `instrument-info.4C.json`, `instrument-info.MAERSK-A.json` and `instrument-info.AALLON.json` are unchanged replies of `/api/nordic/instruments/{orderbookId}/info?assetClass=SHARES` for a Stockholm main-market line (`exchange` "Nasdaq Stockholm"), a First North Sweden line ("First North GM Sweden"), a Copenhagen main-market line ("Nasdaq Copenhagen") and a First North Finland line ("First North GM Finland"); the Helsinki main-market label "Nasdaq Helsinki" and the Copenhagen First North label "First North GM Denmark" were read the same day.
- `instrument-info.AINO.json` is the unchanged reply for a First North Sweden line too illiquid for continuous trading; its `exchange` is "First North GM Sweden Auction", the same venue named with its trading model. A census of all 27 Copenhagen First North lines the same day answered with exactly two labels, "First North GM Denmark" (24) and "First North GM Denmark Auction" (3), so a whole list holds the pair and no third spelling.
- Neither reply states a venue code: the list a line comes from (market plus category) names its MIC, and the instrument reply's exchange label must agree.
